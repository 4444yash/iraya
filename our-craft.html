Our Heritage

Shaped by Reverence, Not Machines

Iraya was born from a singular conviction: that the exquisite mastery of Indian silvercraft deserves a place on the world stage, stripped of ornamental clutter and celebrated for its raw sculptural purity.

The Lineage

Centuries in One Wire

In the silver ateliers of Cuttack (Odisha) and Jaipur (Rajasthan), our artisans trace their craft through four and five generations. A single pair of Tarakasi filigree earrings requires drawing sterling silver through progressively smaller diamond dies until it reaches the thickness of a strand of hair.

Two threads are spun together to create a delicate serrated edge, then hand-crimped into floral, geometric, and architectural motifs without molds or templates.

Ethical Sourcing & Living Wages

We eliminate layers of middlemen to pay our artisan partners 3x the standard regional wage, supporting craft retention and apprenticeships for young silversmiths.
