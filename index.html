Centuries of Reverence

Every curve is bent by hand. No two pieces are ever identical.

"In Cuttack and Jaipur, master silversmiths pull pure silver into threads finer than silk, twisting each scroll with tweezers over a gentle flame."

We work directly with multi-generational artisanal workshops across India, ensuring living ethical wages, safe atelier conditions, and the preservation of rare techniques like Tarakasi (fine wire filigree) and Meenakari (enamel artistry). All pieces are rigorously assayed and stamped 925 sterling silver.
