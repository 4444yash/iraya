Artisanal Pedigree

The Art of Indian Sterling Silver Jewellery

Indian silvercraft dates back over five thousand years to the Indus Valley civilization. Unlike mass-manufactured cast jewelry, each Iraya piece is constructed from high-purity 925 sterling silver alloyed with copper for structural durability, completely free of nickel and lead.

Whether wearing delicate Tarakasi wire earrings to a US business meeting or layering an oxidized silver choker over a cocktail dress, Iraya pieces provide the perfect harmony of heirloom cultural storytelling and contemporary Western minimalism.
